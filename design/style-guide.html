02 · Accessibility

What changed to meet WCAG AA

Text needs 4.5:1 against its background; input borders, rings and icons need 3:1. Your original colors are all kept. Where one failed, it stays for fills and decoration and a darker “ink” takes over for text.

Motion

Presses: 150ms scale. Selections: 200ms. Rings fill over 900ms with a gentle ease-out. Celebrations last under a second and appear only for streak milestones and completed goals. With reduced motion, every animation becomes an instant change.

Shape

Cards 20px, signature surfaces 24–28px, buttons and chips fully rounded. Shadows are soft and tinted with Deep Tide instead of grey.
